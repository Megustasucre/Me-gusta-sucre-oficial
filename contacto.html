Getting Here

From the airport
or bus terminal

All three locations are in the historic center — steps from Plaza 25 de Mayo.

From Alcantarí Airport

~50 min from the city

Official taxi: 80–120 Bs (approx. $12–17 USD)

Shared minibus: 15–20 Bs / person (when available)

From the Bus Terminal

~10 min from the historic center

Taxi: 15–20 Bs (approx. $2–3 USD)

City bus (micro): 2 Bs — ask for the historic center

All three locations are within 400 m of each other in the historic center — a 5-minute walk between any two of them.
