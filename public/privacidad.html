Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 19, 2026

At Me Gusta Sucre, we believe every traveler deserves to feel at home. We're a Bolivian brand rooted in Sucre's historic heart, bringing together language, food, and authentic experiences through Me Gusta Spanish School, Me Gusta Sucre Inn, and Me Gusta Café. Being transparent about how we handle your information is part of that warmth and trust we want to build with you.

1. What We Collect

  • Contact details: your name, phone number, and email — so we can actually reach you.
  • Reservation info: check-in/check-out dates, number of guests, room preferences.
  • Conversations: messages you send us via WhatsApp or our contact form.
  • Website visits: pages you browse, collected anonymously via Google Analytics.

2. How We Use It

  • To answer your questions and take care of your reservation.
  • To support you on WhatsApp — including through our AI assistant when our team is resting.
  • To send you confirmations or follow-ups about your stay or classes.
  • To keep improving the experience we offer every guest.

3. WhatsApp & Our AI Assistant

We use the official WhatsApp Business Platform (Meta Platforms, Inc.) to stay in touch with you. When you write to us, our friendly AI assistant — powered by Groq AI — may reply on our behalf, especially outside office hours. Your messages are used only to help you. We never sell or share your WhatsApp conversations with third parties for advertising.

4. Third-Party Tools We Use

  • Meta / WhatsApp Business API — how your messages reach us.
  • Groq AI — powers our automated WhatsApp assistant.
  • Google Analytics — helps us understand how visitors use our site.
  • Google Maps — so you can find us easily.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy — we encourage you to take a look.

5. How Long We Keep Your Data

We only keep your personal data as long as we genuinely need it — to complete your booking, answer your question, or comply with the law. Our WhatsApp conversation history resets periodically.

6. Your Rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal information at any time. Just reach out — we'll sort it out together:

7. Cookies

Our website uses cookies to understand how people find us and navigate the site (Google Analytics). By browsing megustasucre.com, you're okay with that. You can always turn cookies off in your browser settings.

8. Updates

If we ever update this policy, we'll post the new version here with a fresh date. Continuing to use our services means you're happy with the changes.
